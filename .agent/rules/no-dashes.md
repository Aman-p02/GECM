# Style & Content Rules for GEC Modasa Website

## Punctuation & Typography
- **NEVER use long dashes (em-dash `—`, en-dash `–`, `&mdash;`, `&ndash;`) anywhere in titles, headings, breadcrumbs, descriptions, or placeholders.**
- For titles or department headers, use a simple hyphen with spaces (` - `) or a colon (`: `) (e.g. `Computer Engineering - Newsletters & Periodicals` or `Computer Engineering: Faculty Directory`).
- For empty values/placeholders, use `-` or `N/A`, never `—`.
